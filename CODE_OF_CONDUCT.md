# Code of Conduct

krēCHer is a student project, shared openly so others can learn from it, build on it, or point out what could be better. Everyone who opens an issue, comments, or contributes is welcome here.

## What I ask

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** Many people here, me included, are new to hardware. Questions are welcome, including basic ones.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** Specific, constructive feedback on code, wiring or design is the most useful kind.
- **Respect people's privacy.** Don't post anyone's personal information, and don't share photos or recordings of people without their consent.
- **Take safety seriously.** This project uses LiPo batteries and motors. Don't encourage anything that puts people or property at risk, and point it out if you see something unsafe in the repo.
- **Credit what you use.** If you build on someone else's work, say so, as this repo does in [`BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md).

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or imagery, threats, unwelcome sexual attention, and deliberately disruptive behaviour, in any space connected to this project.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@mawakeita](https://github.com/mawakeita), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately. Comments, issues or contributions that break this code may be edited, hidden or removed, and repeat behaviour may lead to being blocked from the repository.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
