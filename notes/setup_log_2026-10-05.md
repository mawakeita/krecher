## Hip pivot (Robokits Step 4–5): servo won't fit
- The temporary M3 pivot screw (no bearing yet) was **too long**: it stuck into the space where the hip servo body sits.
- Right length = lower plate + bracket wall + washers + nut, plus 1–2 threads.
- Fix used: **flip the screw** (insert it from the servo side, nut on the outside) so the extra length points away from the servo. It worked. Better-fitting screws and washers are on order.

## Wiring plan
- **Order matters:** hip servos sit between the plates, so every leg's leads must be routed through the plate gap **before the top plate goes on**.
- **Per leg:** knee lead along the underside of the link → joins the femur lead at the hip bracket → in between the plates at that leg's lobe → joins the hip lead → to the Servo 2040.
- **Slack loops** (~3–4 cm, a gentle U) at the knee, femur and hip (the hip loop must allow ~±30–45° swing). No cable across a horn, pivot screw or plate edge.
- **Extensions (30 cm):** dry-fit each lead first. Tape or heat-shrink every extension joint.
- **Labels:** a second tape flag with the channel number at the board end of every lead. Bundle each leg's leads in spiral wrap from the hip outward, not across the joint loops.
- **Plugging in:** channel N = header N on the silkscreen (code index N−1). Brown = GND, red = V+, orange = signal.

## Head placement and body layout (decided)
- Head at the **back, facing forward**; **fixed head** for now (pan-tilt does the looking; room for a neck servo later). **3S LiPo at the front** as the counterweight. The **2S head battery stays in the head**, so the head stays a self-contained unit.
- The back slot sits under the head, so it carries the Pi → Servo 2040 USB cable and the I2C cable to the BNO085. For now, unplugging the XT60 at the front is the on/off switch.
- **BNO085 goes on the body**, not the head: it measures the body directly and stays right if a neck is added later.
- **Balance:** centre of mass between the middle hips or slightly forward (it has to stay inside the 3-foot triangle while walking). Check by balancing the finished body on a pencil.
- **Fixing:** temporary (velcro or zip ties) until the balance check; bolt down after the first walk.
