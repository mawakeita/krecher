## Power prep (done)
- **Servo 2040 link cut:** the small gold "dumbbell" pad top-right on the back, beside "Separate USB & Ext. Power", scored across the thin middle. Re-test on USB from the Pi: `lsusb` shows `2e8a:0005 MicroPython Board in FS mode`, `/dev/ttyACM0` present, `mpremote ... exec` → **`servo2040 OK 18`**. From now on the board needs USB (logic) + battery (servos).
- **Buck voltage:** 5.80 → **6.00 V**.
- **Buck current limit:** **3.0 A** (limit turned to minimum first, then raised while measuring on the meter's 10 A socket; meter put back to volts afterwards).
- **Buck → Servo 2040:** the board's power screw terminal is too small for 14 AWG wire (~2 h of trying). Workaround: **two male jumper wires per side**, pins straight into the terminal (2 red → +, 2 black → −). Measured at the terminal: **+6.01 V**. Fine for one leg at a time at the 3 A limit; check the jumpers don't get hot.
- **To buy:** 18 AWG (or 16 AWG) silicone wire for the buck → Servo 2040 run before standing or walking with all 18 servos.

## Decisions
- Foot-switch wires: run all 6 now (2-wire cable, ~40 cm per foot, beside each knee lead with the same loops). Switches mounted on the feet later.
- Ultrasonic at the front of the body, facing forward; 4-wire lead between the plates to the back slot, up to the HAT (pins 27 + 22).
- Head-side pin plan (Fusion HAT+): touch module pin 17, motion sensor (PIR) pin 4, ultrasonic 27 + 22; OLED + BNO085 on the two I2C ports (OLED 0x3C, IMU 0x4A, HAT 0x17); mood lights on the RGB port; pan/tilt PWM 2/3 (P0/P1 free for a neck servo). Joystick = bench tool only.
- Servo 2040: 18/18 servo channels (legs), 6/6 sensor headers (feet). Full. Foot switches: COM → signal, NO → 3.3 V; foot down reads ~3.3 V.
- Install order: inside-body parts before the top plate → BNO085 → OLED → mood lights → touch → PIR → ultrasonic; each tested alone on the bench first.
- No kitchen scale: balance by the pencil test (body balanced at the middle legs or slightly forward).

## Layout change: battery and Servo 2040 between the plates (test-fitted)
- **3S LiPo at the front**, lengthwise between the FL and FR hips, leads exiting forward. Still the counterweight to the head at the back.
- **Servo 2040 in the middle**, USB-C facing the back (reachable between the RL and RR hips); servo headers reachable through the top plate's centre square. Fixed with double-sided foam tape, which also insulates its solder points from the aluminium.
- Replaces the Oct 5 plan (LiPo + buck + Servo 2040 on the top plate).
- Top plate, front → back: buck converter (open air) → open centre square → head over the slot. BNO085 near the middle. 1–2 cm air gap between the buck's heatsink and the head. The buck is never inside the head (3S wiring stays away from the HAT).
- Before fixing: swing each leg by hand to check the hip servos clear the battery and the Servo 2040; check a plugged-in lead bends under the top plate; velcro strap + soft layer under the LiPo.
