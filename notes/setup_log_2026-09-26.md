# Setup log: 2026-09-26 (Saturday, Sprints 4–6)

## Sprint 4: BNO085 wiring + first reading (done)
- **Wiring:** no soldering. The Fusion HAT+ has a 4-pin I2C port (labels GND · 3V3 · SDA · SCL, bottom-left corner near the mounting screw, under the camera ribbon). A 4-wire cable from the SunFounder kit fits both that port and the BNO085's STEMMA QT port. Either BNO085 port works (they are wired together). Header strip left unsoldered.
- **I2C bus:** baseline `i2cdetect -y 1` showed only `17` as `UU` (the HAT's own chip, claimed by its driver). With the BNO085 connected: `4a` appears. No address conflict.
- **Software:** venv at `~/venvs/imu` (`--system-site-packages`), with `adafruit-circuitpython-bno08x` 1.3.3 and `adafruit-blinka` 9.2.0. Activate with `source ~/venvs/imu/bin/activate`.
- **Script:** `exp02/imu_read.py` (commit db70e60) enables the rotation vector report, converts the quaternion to roll/pitch/yaw in degrees, prints at 10 Hz.
- **Result:** works with the default I2C speed; no errors, no freezes (the known Pi clock-stretching problem did not appear). Roll, pitch and yaw all respond to tilting and turning; yaw wraps at ±180° as expected. At rest the values settle and drift very slowly (about 0.5° over ~10 s while the fusion settles).

## Sprint 6: head follows the IMU (done)
- `exp02/imu_head_follow.py`: home = pose at start; tilt = pitch (±20°), pan = wrapped yaw (±30°); smoothing 0.25, 0.5° deadband, 20 Hz; centres on Ctrl+C. `fusion_hat` imports fine inside the venv thanks to `--system-site-packages`.
- Works: first sense → react loop. Limits hold; smoothing visible. Quirk: pan flips side when the sensor faces ~180° from home.
