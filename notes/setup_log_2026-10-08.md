## Keeping the model warm: `system/keep_warm.sh`
- Notes on the D12 GPU box: Ollama unloads a model after 30 idle minutes, and bigger models on the same 16 GB card can push gemma4 out, so a cold start can take over 2 s. The Pi only gets through while the box is checked out (1-hour leases by default). Going through the hotspot's relay is normal; 0.35 s warm is fine. Reflexes like the flinch stay on the Pi.
- `system/keep_warm.sh`: every 20 min sends `{"model":"gemma4:e4b","keep_alive":"30m"}` (no prompt) to the box. Prints `HH:MM warm`, or `NO ANSWER: check your lease` if the box refuses. Stop with Ctrl+C. **Tested with Box 2 checked out: `warm`.**
- Demo day: check out the box, run the script, leave the terminal open; renew the lease if the demo runs past an hour.
- There's no voice code yet (the Oct 1 test was by hand with `curl`). When it's written, its requests to Ollama should include `"keep_alive": "30m"`.

## Milestone: krēCHer stands up and sits down
- **Bare body only** (battery, Servo 2040, buck, Pi and head on the bench, cables to the body). Jumper power, 3 A limit not raised.
- **Direction test** `body/dir_test.py` (+20° on one femur and one knee per side):
  - FL femur (ch 2) +20 → knee end **up**; FL knee (ch 3) +20 → foot **in**.
  - FR femur (ch 11) +20 → knee end **down**; FR knee (ch 12) +20 → foot **out**.
  - The sides are exact mirrors; ML/RL behave like FL, MR/RR like FR.
- **`body/stand_up.py`:** `FEMUR_DOWN = {"L": -1, "R": 1}`, `KNEE_OUT = {"L": -1, "R": 1}`; pose(θ): femur = FEMUR_DOWN·θ, knee = KNEE_OUT·θ (the knee cancels the femur tilt so the calf stays upright); hips 0. CROUCH = −25°, STAND = +10°, 1° per 0.08 s ≈ 3 s each way. Crouch one servo at a time → stand → hold → sit → limp.
- First try: the legs lifted but the body didn't rise because **the support boxes were too tall**. One box removed, no code change. Rule: boxes hold the body ~2–3 cm below standing height.
- **Three clean runs**, the last with a **30 s hold**.
- `body/stand_hold.py` (all 18 to middle, hold 20 s) is superseded by `stand_up.py`.
