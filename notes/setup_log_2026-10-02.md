## Robokits Step 4: bearings not in the pack
- Step 4: lower shell + two Step 3 bracket structures + 2 × M3 screws, 2 × nuts, 2 × miniature ball bearings. Servos go in at Step 5. 6 bearings in total for six legs; the guide gives no size.
- What it is: the hip's back pivot. The hip servo's shaft turns on the horn in the upper plate; directly below it, an M3 screw through the bracket and a bearing in the lower plate holds the other side.
- This kit probably doesn't include bearings (the Robokits guide is for a different kit). To do: measure the lower plate's pivot hole and order 3 mm bore miniature bearings.
- Meanwhile: plain M3 screw + nut as the pivot (snug, still turns freely, nylon lock nut or threadlocker).
