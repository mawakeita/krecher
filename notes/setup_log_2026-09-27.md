## Servo tester wiring
- HJ tester header: 5 groups of 3 pins (IN, OUT 1–4). With the tester face up: **S on top, + middle, − bottom** (confirmed from an HJ tester video; the continuity test couldn't tell, since all pins beeped).
- Buck OUT+ → IN middle pin, OUT− → IN bottom pin. Servo into OUT 1: yellow (signal) up, red middle, brown down. These MG996Rs use brown / red / yellow.
